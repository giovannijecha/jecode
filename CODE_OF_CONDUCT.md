# Code of Conduct

Jecode's community spaces exist to make the product more useful, understandable,
and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, direct, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and behavior, not people.
- Keep conversations relevant and give others room to participate.
- Protect privacy: never expose credentials, private code, personal data, or
  someone else's conversation transcript.
- Accept moderation decisions and move on.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption,
spam, and publishing private information are not welcome.

## Reporting and enforcement

Use GitHub's report and block controls for abusive public content. For a private
conduct concern involving the repository, contact the maintainer through their
GitHub profile without including sensitive project data in a public thread.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and restrict participation
when behavior works against a safe and useful community.
